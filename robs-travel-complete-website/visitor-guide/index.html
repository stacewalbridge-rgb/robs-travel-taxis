Discover Bude & North Cornwall

A practical guide for visitors planning where to eat, where to stay, what to see and how to get around Bude and the surrounding coast and countryside.

Bude

Bude is a North Cornwall seaside town known for its beaches, sea pool, canal, independent food businesses and access to the South West Coast Path. Rob's Travel provides pre-booked local journeys, airport transfers and railway connections for visitors staying in and around town.
