Privacy Policy.

How Rob's Travel Taxis handles information provided through our website and customer booking app.

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Rob's Travel Taxis provides taxi, airport transfer and long-distance booking services. This policy explains how information is collected, used and protected when you use our website or customer booking app.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, pickup and destination details, journey date and time, passenger and luggage information, booking notes, device or technical information required to operate the service, and location information when you choose to use location-based booking features.

How we use information

We use information to provide fare estimates, create and manage bookings, contact you about a journey, dispatch drivers, provide customer support, maintain security and reliability, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Location data

If you choose to allow location access, the app may use your device location to help identify a pickup point or support booking-related location features. Location access is used only for providing the requested transport service and associated app functions.

Sharing information

Information may be shared only where needed to operate your booking, including with the assigned driver and service providers that support hosting, maps, routing, communications and app operation. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for bookings, customer service, accounting, legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention and security purposes.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information where applicable under UK data-protection law. You may also withdraw optional permissions such as device location through your phone settings.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Children

The app is intended for customers arranging transport and is not designed as a service directed specifically at children. A parent, guardian or responsible adult should make bookings for children where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Rob's Travel Taxis by telephone on 07771 824141 or through the contact options on robs-travel.co.uk/contact/.
