FAQ

WhyDrive, without the jargon.

The platform is being built to complement licensed local transport — not pretend every market works like a major-city ride-hailing network.

Is WhyDrive itself a taxi company?

WhyDrive is being developed as a technology and booking network connecting customers with participating licensed taxi and private hire drivers and operators.

Do drivers race to tap jobs first?

No. Smart Dispatch is designed to assess eligible drivers first and offer the booking to the selected driver.

How does fair allocation work?

Eligibility comes first. Where several drivers are similarly suitable, eligible workload and idle time can help balance the next opportunity.

What about airport and long-distance work?

Major jobs can be considered separately from short local work, with compatible return-direction demand used to reduce empty mileage where practical.
