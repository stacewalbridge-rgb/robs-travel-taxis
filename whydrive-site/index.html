Why WhyDrive?

Technology should strengthen local transport — not replace it.

WhyDrive is designed to make it easier for customers to find a ride and easier for licensed local drivers to work together without giving up the independence that makes local taxi and private hire services valuable.

⌖

Local people find local drivers

Customers can request a journey through one simple app or web booking experience instead of hunting around for who might be available.

✦

Smart matching, not a free-for-all

WhyDrive checks availability, vehicle suitability, position, timing and fairness before allocating work — instead of broadcasting every job to everyone.

↺

Fewer wasted miles

Smart Return Matching can identify compatible return-direction work after major long-distance jobs, helping drivers reduce expensive empty runs.

Questions

WhyDrive in plain English.

Is WhyDrive a taxi company?

WhyDrive is being built as a technology and booking network that connects customers with participating licensed taxi and private hire drivers. Local licensing and the legal status of each journey still matter.

Do drivers have to race to accept jobs?

No. WhyDrive’s Smart Dispatch approach is designed to choose the suitable driver first and send the offer to that driver instead of creating a fastest-finger broadcast race.

How does WhyDrive try to be fair?

Eligibility comes first — availability, vehicle, timing, position and conflicts. Where several drivers are similarly suitable, eligible workload and idle time can help balance the next allocation.

What about airport and long-distance work?

Major long-distance journeys can use their own fairness queue, and compatible return-direction bookings can be matched to help reduce empty return mileage where practical.

Will there be an app?

The WhyDrive experience is designed for iPhone, Android and web use. Store availability and area-by-area launch dates will be announced as rollout progresses.
